## Dek

Pick the right path before you book flights. Most Indian founders land on one of four: a B-1 to scout, an O-1A to build, an L-1A if you already run a real Indian company, or an H-1B if you already hold one.

## Sections on this page

- O-1A: the founder default
- L-1A: if you already run a company in India
- H-1B: the founder rule changed in 2025
- Think green card from day one
